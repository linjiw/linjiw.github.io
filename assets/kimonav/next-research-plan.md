# M3r: qualify tracker adaptation and retention before training

Next-stage draft after M3q, 6 September 2026. No M3r fine-tuning, vectorized
physics or hardware is authorized by this document alone; the research task
continues with read-only qualification and a finite prospective protocol.

## Why change the intervention

M3o selection cannot create missing candidate headroom: no fresh pivot–walk–stop
candidate succeeds. M3p's unconstrained response model fails free prediction.
M3q's bounded preview reduces predictive composite by 19.41%, but fails its
heading guard. Neither response model is a validated compensation teacher.
Keep those results fixed rather than changing a gate or launching distillation.

Test Fable's remaining hypothesis: the tracking policy can benefit from
practice on generated navigation motions, while retaining broad motion tracking.
This changes SONIC; ARDY remains frozen. The resulting claim would be
navigation-specific tracker adaptation with measured retention cost, not a
fully frozen controller or a novel ARDY/SONIC integration.

## First work: zero gradient updates and zero new physics

Inventory the pinned SONIC training entrypoint, release checkpoint compatibility,
motion-library converters, training observation/reward/termination configuration,
and available human-motion retention data. Establish which assets actually exist
locally and their licenses/source identities. Do not download a large dataset or
launch the default thousands-environment training configuration on assumption.

Create separate manifests for proposed navigation practice and retention
evaluation. The 24 development contexts and eight draws stay grouped; the twelve
reserved programs remain sealed. Generated kinematic references may be practice
data even when tracking failed, but they must satisfy the declared reference
contract. Do not relabel a failed execution as successful demonstration motion.
Keep actual outcomes/termination causes as labels and report the selection rule.

Audit availability of a genuine retention pool beyond the narrow navigation
program bank. A motion prior's quality is not a substitute for retention
measurement. If a retention set is missing, identifying that exact dependency
is the deliverable before adaptation; do not silently omit the comparator.

## Protocol to finalize after qualification

Specify frozen-tracker baseline, matched navigation-only adaptation, and
navigation-plus-retention adaptation. Use the same update/compute budget and
initial checkpoint. Declare family splits, practice mixing, retention metrics,
task tolerances, failure accounting and a finite update/physics budget before
training. Qualify multi-environment captures and clocks against the established
single-environment replay contract before interpreting speedups or batched
outcomes. Any residual-policy alternative needs its own explicitly registered arm.

Report executed original-clock success, path/heading errors, STOP behavior,
fall/termination and foot-slide/jerk alongside retention. Success filtering,
training return or predictor MSE alone cannot promote the controller. Only after
matched development evidence should the frozen chosen method access independent
confirmation, new simulator seeds, repeated measured feedback and live deadlines.
